Our Story — The Full CCP Journey

Creative Cardboard and Paper started on 16 July 2026 with a very simple beginning: a few handmade paper products, a lot of ideas and the excitement of creating something of our own. We did not begin with a huge catalogue or a complicated business plan. We began by making things, showing them to people and seeing what they liked.

Our first stage was all about stationery. Customized pens were one of our earliest products, followed by mini notebooks, binders and bookmarks. We experimented with different ways of making notebooks, including eco-friendly toothpick binding and modern stapler binding. We learned that even a small product needs attention to design, quality, customization and presentation.

As more people became interested, CCP started becoming more than a stationery project. We began creating football collectible cards, including themed sets and different rarity levels. The idea was to make the cards feel like something people could collect, trade and talk about rather than just ordinary pieces of paper. This was one of the moments when CCP started thinking seriously about building a recognizable identity.

Another major step was our craft range. We added handmade paper display crafts such as the paper dagger, paper kunai, paper katana and paper knife. They are made from paper and are sold as creative display crafts. Each one can be presented as part of the CCP handmade collection, and we can continue improving the designs and customization options as we learn more.

Our team has also changed as the business has grown. People have taken on different responsibilities across sales, advertising, management, technology, games and craft. Arinjay leads the business and marketing, Riyansh helps with advertising and the creative side, and Aadit is now Head of Games & Craft. Team members have joined, moved roles and left as the project developed. Those changes are part of building something together and learning what each person is best at.

We have also learned from real orders. Customers have bought pens, notebooks, binders, bookmarks, football cards and paper crafts. Every order has helped us understand pricing, customization, customer service and keeping proper records. Even small sales matter because they show us that an idea made by a small team can become something other people actually want.

Our achievements are not only about money or the number of products. We have built a product catalogue, created a website, developed our own branding, introduced collectible cards, expanded into handmade crafts, and learned how to run different parts of a small business. We have made mistakes too, but each mistake gives us something to improve.

The next chapter of CCP is about improving what already works. We want cleaner product pages, better photos, more customization choices, new card sets, more handmade products and a stronger community around the brand. We want every product to feel like it was made with care.

That is what Creative Cardboard means to us: start small, make something with your own hands, learn from every customer, keep improving and turn an idea into something real.
