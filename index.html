Our Story

From cardboard to a real little business.

Creative Cardboard & Paper started on 16 July 2026 with a simple idea: turn ordinary paper and cardboard into things people would actually enjoy. We started small, experimented with products and slowly built a business around creativity.

Our journey began with handmade stationery such as customized pens, notebooks, binders and bookmarks. Every order taught us something — how to make products better, how to price them, how to listen to customers and how to keep improving.

We then explored football collectibles and handmade paper crafts. The football-card idea introduced sets, ratings and collecting, while our craft range gave us another way to turn paper into creative display pieces.

The most important part of CCP is the team. People have helped with marketing, advertising, technology, games, craft and sales. We have changed roles as we have grown, and every change has taught us more about teamwork and responsibility.

We are proud of every order, every review and every new idea. CCP is still growing, but that is the fun of it: start small, create something, learn from it and make the next version better.
